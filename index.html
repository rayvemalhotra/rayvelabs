RayveNet
● Live

An interactive network-security walkthrough. Three labs and a self-service toolkit inside one tool. Scan your network lab teaches discovery — vantage point, existence vs. identity, completeness vs. soundness — and includes Simulation 1 against a known-ground-truth network. Cascade Lab teaches device fingerprinting via four self-service tools (MAC OUI, realm decoder, banner analyzer, category-error spotter) and a difficulty-graded game. Web Log Lab teaches privacy-preserving request fingerprinting from raw Apache/Nginx access logs — parse, normalize, SHA-256, compare. Everything runs client-side.
