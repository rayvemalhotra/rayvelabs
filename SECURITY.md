# Security Policy

RayveLabs is a small open research practice in cybersecurity. We take security
issues seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure.

## Scope

In scope:
- The website https://rayvelabs.com (and its content, JavaScript, and
  client-side logic, including the RayveNet labs at /rayvenet.html)
- This repository (https://github.com/rayvemalhotra/rayvelabs)

Out of scope:
- Anything on third-party domains we link to (LinkedIn, fonts.googleapis.com,
  GitHub, etc.)
- Denial-of-service testing
- Social-engineering or physical attacks
- Issues in deprecated browsers (we target modern evergreen browsers only)

## Reporting

Email security issues privately to **rayvemalhotra@gmail.com**.

If the report contains sensitive material, encrypt it (PGP key available on
request) before sending, or share via a one-time secret service.

When reporting, please include:

1. A clear description of the issue and where you found it (URL or file).
2. Steps to reproduce, with the smallest possible test case.
3. Impact you observed or suspect.
4. Whether the issue is already public anywhere.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within **5 business days**.
- A working assessment within 10 business days.
- Credit in the fix commit and a mention here, if you want it.

## Public disclosure

Please do not disclose the issue publicly until either (a) we have shipped a
fix, or (b) 90 days have passed since you reported, whichever comes first.
We will coordinate the disclosure timeline with you.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against good-faith security research that:
- Follows this policy
- Avoids privacy violations, destruction of data, and interruption of service
- Stops as soon as the issue is acknowledged
- Does not exfiltrate data beyond the minimum needed to demonstrate the issue
