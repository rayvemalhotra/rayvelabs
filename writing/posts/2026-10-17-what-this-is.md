Most of what I build at RayveLabs ships as something you can open and use: a lab, a tool, a simulator. This section is for the things that don't fit in a tool — the ideas behind them.

The rule is simple. **Short, honest, by topic.** A note on why a discovery scan can tell you a device *exists* but not what it *is*. A sketch of how a digital twin should lose its authority to approve a change. A practical take on post-quantum migration for people who have to actually do it.

Nothing here is a paper. If it grows into one, it'll link out. Until then, this is the working notebook, in public.
